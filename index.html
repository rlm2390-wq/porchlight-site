Questions owners ask

Do you need my passwords?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile and a user on Yelp for Business. Both take a couple of minutes, and you can remove us anytime.

Will the replies sound like me?

That’s the whole point. You answer a few quick questions about how you talk to customers, and for the first two weeks you see every reply before it goes up.

What about bad reviews?

We answer calmly, never argue, and invite the customer to reach you directly. Anything about safety, health, legal issues, or a named employee comes to you first.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. Fake reviews are against federal law and against Google’s and Yelp’s rules. We only reply to real ones and help you ask every customer for theirs.

Do you handle social media?

We stick to reviews, Google, and Yelp, since that’s where people decide whether to visit. We don’t run Instagram or Facebook posting. Answering Facebook recommendations is available if you ask.

Is there a contract?

Month to month. Stop whenever you like, and everything we made for you stays yours.
